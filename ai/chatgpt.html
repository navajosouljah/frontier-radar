The Iranian campaign ran seven fake journalist personas that pitched articles to outlets worldwide. The Russian campaign used unwitting people in Latin America to front a fake think tank. OpenAI rated them Category 4 and 5 on its IO Breakout Scale. Source

OpenAI publishes 722 machine-written math manuscripts from an unreleased model
The papers, grouped into 372 result families, come from an internal model evaluated on about 4,000 open problems. Many proofs ship as Lean formalizations in a public GitHub repo. OpenAI says it is working to release the model responsibly. Source

Powered by GPT-6 Luna, it returns yes/no, choice or score answers over text and images, up to 10x faster than a normal GPT-6 call, and charges only for input. Simon Willison notes the shape closely matches Jev, which launched three weeks earlier. Source
