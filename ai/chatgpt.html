Model Oct 6
OpenAI publishes 722 machine-written math manuscripts from an unreleased model
The papers, grouped into 372 result families, come from an internal model evaluated on about 4,000 open problems. Many proofs ship as Lean formalizations in a public GitHub repo. OpenAI says it is working to release the model responsibly. Source
Feature Oct 6
OpenAI's Decisions API enters public beta, a typed-answer rival to TypeSafe's Jev
Powered by GPT-6 Luna, it returns yes/no, choice or score answers over text and images, up to 10x faster than a normal GPT-6 call, and charges only for input. Simon Willison notes the shape closely matches Jev, which launched three weeks earlier. Source
Apps & integrations Oct 6
Atlassian expands its OpenAI deal: GPT-6 models will power agents across Jira, Confluence and Rovo
Under the new agreement OpenAI frontier models, including GPT-6 Astra and the GPT-5.6 series, run Atlassian's Rovo agents on top of its Teamwork Graph. More than 3,000 Atlassian developers already use Codex. Source
Feature Oct 6
OpenAI trains computer-use agents on Ironclad's contracting software
GPT-6 Astra is the first model trained on 11 real legal-ops tasks built with Ironclad. On OpenAI's evaluation it scored 55% against 41.6% for GPT-5.6 Sol, in about half the time. OpenAI is inviting a few more software companies to join. Source
Policy, safety & outages Oct 6
A rough day for OpenAI's status page: 11 incidents on Oct 6
Incidents ran from elevated errors across ChatGPT, Codex and the API, including the Agents API, to Computer Use problems and large-PDF failures in the Responses API. Workspace Agents were degraded again early on Oct 7. Source
Feature Oct 6
ChatGPT can now take audio uploads and turn meetings into transcripts and notes
Paid ChatGPT plans and workspaces can attach an audio file and ask for a transcript, a summary or a follow-up draft. OpenAI warns transcripts may contain errors and quality varies by language. Source
Policy, safety & outages Oct 5
OpenAI will watermark ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU, opt-in for API customers worldwide
Its textGrain method adds an invisible statistical signal to word choices. OpenAI says detection drops sharply on short or edited text, so only approved researchers get the detector for now. Image and audio verification stays public. Source
