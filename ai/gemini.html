Report: Google internally tests Gemini 4 checkpoint Carbon, said to match Opus 5.5 in coding
Business Insider reports that employees are testing a new Gemini 4 build codenamed Carbon on the internal coding platform Jetski. One tester compared it to Opus 5.5, but some say the model is less convincing in practice. Source
Pricing & plans Oct 9
Free Gemini users are limited to Flash-Lite from October 9; AI Plus subscribers lose Pro access
Google restricted the free tier to the Flash-Lite model only, removing the Flash and Pro model pickers. AI Plus ($4.99/month) keeps Flash-Lite and Flash but loses Pro. AI Pro and Ultra keep all models and gain Deep Think. Source
Feature Oct 8
Google launches the Gemini agent: one AI that plans, delegates, and works across enterprise systems
Announced at Gemini at Work 2026, the agent gets its own Workspace email, calendar, and Drive. It picks a model automatically (including Claude), connects to Slack, Jira, and databases, and can spawn sub-agents. Businesses first, consumers later. Source
Model Oct 6
Nano Banana 2.1: Google's fast image model gets sharper output, 14 reference images and a lower price
The update to Nano Banana 2 improves realism at 1K to 4K, fixes tiling on panoramic ratios, renders text better and adds web grounding. Logan Kilpatrick says it ships in the API, AI Studio and the Gemini app at a new lower price point. Source
Model Oct 6
Google releases EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M open model that embeds text, images, audio, video and code
The Apache 2.0 model runs on-device, needs about 191 MB of RAM and scores 78.68 on the MTEB code benchmark, up nearly 10 points on its predecessor. Weights are on Hugging Face and Kaggle. Source
